Latest Updates

  • The NCCoE is hosting a webinar on December 10, 2026: NIST CSF 2.0 Semiconductor Manufacturing Profile Kick-Off Webinar
  • NIST has published Cybersecurity White Paper 50, Small Business Cybersecurity: Non-Employer Firms. The report is designed to help small businesses use the NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0 to manage their cybersecurity risks. The document is tailored to the smallest of businesses — those with no employees other than the owner, or “non-employer” firms as defined by the U.S. Small Business Administration.
  • Seeking public comments until December 7, 2026: NIST Special Publication (SP) 800-221r1 ipd (Revision 1, Initial Public draft), Enterprise Impact of Information and Communications Technology (ICT) Risk: Governing and Managing ICT Risk Programs Within an Enterprise Risk Portfolio. SP 800-221r1 ipd helps organizations improve their ICTRM in alignment with established frameworks, including the CSF 2.0.
  • Available for public comment: CSF 2.0 Community Profile for Federal Agency Open Radio Access Network (O-RAN) Deployment. This draft CSF 2.0 profile describes how components that conform to the security specifications produced by the O-RAN ALLIANCE support various CSF 2.0 outcomes.
  • The final version of CSF 2.0 Informative References Quick‑Start Guide has published. This document explains how to find, filter, and apply informative references using NIST tools. The document also provides two sample use cases along with examples of how super intelligence (SI) tools can support reference data use when implemented with continuous evaluation and improvement.
